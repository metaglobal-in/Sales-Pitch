Gym Website Cold Call Pitch - Hinglish Script

Use for gym owners who are active on Google/Instagram/Justdial but do not have a strong website. Tone: respectful, confident, problem-solving. Language mix: 60-70% Hindi, 30-40% English.

1. Call Goal

Main goal: Owner ko immediately website sell nahi karni. Pehle unko ye realise karwana hai ki unke gym ki online enquiry leak ho rahi hai.

Call ka target: 10-15 minute ka demo/meeting book karna jahan hum unko dikha sakein ki website se trials, WhatsApp enquiries aur memberships kaise badh sakti hain.

2. Opening Hook - Client Ko Rokne Wali Line

Salesperson: Namaste sir, kya main [Gym Name] ke owner/manager se baat kar raha hoon?

Owner: Haan boliye.

Salesperson: Sir main [Your Name] bol raha hoon. Main aapko website bechne ke liye direct call nahi kar raha. Actually maine aapka gym online dekha - rating kaafi achhi hai aur reviews bhi strong hain. Bas ek chhoti si gap notice hui: jo log aapka gym join karna chahte hain, unko pricing, timing, trial, trainer aur facilities ki complete information ek jagah nahi mil rahi.

Salesperson: Sir 30 seconds dijiye, agar point relevant na lage to main call cut kar dunga.

3. Problem Framing - Owner Ko Pain Samjhao

Hook ke baad turant website ki baat mat karo. Pehle customer behavior samjhao.

Sir aaj kal gym join karne se pehle log seedha online search karte hain: "best gym near me", "gym fees", "personal trainer available hai kya", "ladies batch hai kya", "trial milega kya".

Customer ka mindset ye hota hai ki bina call kiye unki basic enquiry solve ho jaye. Agar unko details clear nahi milti, to wo next gym ke Instagram, Google listing ya website par chale jaate hain.

Aapka gym achha hai, reviews bhi achhe hain, par agar online information scattered hai - thoda Google pe, thoda Instagram pe, thoda Justdial pe - to serious enquiry ka trust drop ho jata hai.

4. Discovery Questions

In questions se owner ko khud bolne do. Jab owner bolta hai, close easy hota hai.

  • Sir abhi zyada enquiries aapko Google se aati hain ya Instagram/Justdial se?
  • Daily approx kitne log fees, timing ya trial ke liye call/WhatsApp karte hain?
  • Kya aapke paas ek proper page hai jahan membership plans, facilities, trainers, batches aur location sab ek jagah visible ho?
  • Aap trial session ya offer run karte hain? Uske liye koi online form/booking system hai?
  • Sir kabhi aisa hota hai ki staff same questions repeat karta rehta hai: fees kya hai, time kya hai, trainer hai kya?

5. Main Pitch - Website Ki Importance

Sir website ka kaam sirf online visiting card banana nahi hai. Website ka kaam hai enquiry ko membership ke close tak le jana.

Example ke liye, jab koi person gym search karta hai, usko 5 cheezein instantly chahiye hoti hain: gym kaisa dikhta hai, fees/trial ka idea, timing, trainers/facilities, aur WhatsApp/call button. Agar ye sab ek professional website pe mil jaye, to customer ka trust badhta hai aur wo enquiry karne ke liye ready hota hai.

Aapka Instagram awareness ke liye achha hai, Google listing discovery ke liye achhi hai, but website conversion ke liye hoti hai. Website ek permanent sales person ki tarah 24x7 kaam karti hai.

7. Demo/Meeting Close

Salesperson: Sir mujhe lagta hai aapke gym ke liye website ka scope strong hai because aapki rating/reviews already achhe hain. Humein bas us trust ko proper online system me convert karna hai.

Salesperson: Main aapko 10 minute ka ek free demo dikha sakta hoon: aapke gym ke naam se homepage kaisa lagega, trial booking button kahan hoga, WhatsApp enquiry kaise aayegi, aur Google search ke liye kya pages ban sakte hain.

Salesperson: Sir aaj shaam 5 baje better rahega ya kal 12 baje?

8. Price Discussion Se Pehle

Agar owner pooche "kitna lagega?", direct discount mat do. Pehle scope set karo.

Sir price exact tabhi bataunga jab 5 minute mein aapka requirement samajh loon. Basic gym website, premium lead-generation website, aur SEO website - teeno ka scope alag hota hai.

But simple language mein, hum aapko aisa website denge jo sirf beautiful nahi, business-oriented ho: WhatsApp leads, call button, Google map, plans, trainer profiles, reviews, gallery aur offer page.

10. Strong Closing Lines

  • Sir aap gym already achha chala rahe ho. Website ka role hai jo trust offline hai, usko online visible banana.
  • Aapke reviews proof hain ki gym quality hai. Ab bas us proof ko ek professional system mein convert karna hai.
  • Sir competitor se pehle customer ka doubt solve karna hi online closing hai.
  • Website ka purpose design nahi, enquiry aur membership conversion hai.

12. WhatsApp Follow-Up Message

Namaste sir, main [Your Name] from [Company Name]. Aaj call par gym website ke regarding baat hui thi.

Quick summary: aapke gym ke reviews/rating strong hain, but website se aap online visitors ko direct enquiry/trial booking mein convert kar sakte hain.

Website mein ye sections rahenge: membership plans, timings, trainers, facilities, gallery, Google reviews, location map, WhatsApp/call button, trial booking form.

Demo ke liye aapka convenient time confirm kar dijiye: today evening ya tomorrow afternoon?

13. Sales Team Rules

  • Call start mein "website banwani hai kya?" kabhi mat bolna.
  • Pehle unki rating/reviews ki genuine appreciation karo.
  • Problem ko customer behavior se connect karo: online search, quick answers, trust, conversion.
  • Meeting close hamesha two-option close se karo: "aaj 5 baje ya kal 12 baje?"
  • Website ko expense nahi, monthly memberships ka conversion system position karo.
